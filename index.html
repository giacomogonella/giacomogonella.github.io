ggonella@fbk.eu

I am a PhD student in Information Engineering and Computer Science based at Fondazione Bruno Kessler, in collaboration with the University of Trento. My research investigates crisis communication through Natural Language Generation, moving from broad, general messages to more precise and personalized communication.

Previously, I earned my BSc in Information Engineering and my MSc in Computer Engineering from the University of Padua, where I completed a thesis on the exploration of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) under the supervision of Prof. Giorgio Satta and Prof. Loris Nanni.
